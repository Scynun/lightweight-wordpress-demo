Your enquiry form is losing customers — 4 small fixes that work

Too many fields, no WhatsApp option, no reply promise. Four changes we make on every client site that lift enquiries fast.

1. Ask for 3 fields, not 8. Name, phone, and what they need. Every extra field costs you enquiries — you can ask the rest on the call.

2. Add a WhatsApp button next to the form. Many visitors would rather tap WhatsApp than fill in a form. Give them the choice.

3. Promise a reply time. “We reply within one working day” under the button sets an expectation and builds trust.

4. Show a clear confirmation. After sending, the visitor should see “Thank you — we’ll call you back” instead of a blank page. A confirmation also stops double submissions.

These are the defaults on every site we build — see them in action on our own contact page.
